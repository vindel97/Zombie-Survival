IN-GAME FEATURES

  • Characters

    Throughout the game, the player is assigned two characters. In Level 1 and 2, the same character is used. To provide a better experience for the player, Level 3 has a different character. However, both characters have similar designs, that makes it appealing for the player. Stay tuned and follow us on X for future updates on Zombie Survival's characters.

  • Enemies

    Plenty of time was invested in the design and coding for the zombies of the game. The initial idea was to make the zombies stronger, scarier and bigger throughout each level. Level 1, showcases two regular zombies taking their heads off. Level 2, inspires more fear, even if its only one zombie, it is bigger and not so easy to defeat. Finally, on Level 3, the player experiences the fear of the Boss Zombies. Three giant and strong zombies ready to eat brains and infest humanity. See more details of how the zombies look in the Gallery section.

  • Backgrounds/Landscapes

    When we started designing the game, our goal was to showcase different scenarios for each level, making it more visually appealing. To design each level background, we got inspired in three major cities around the world. Starting with LAS VEGAS in Level 1. NEW YORK CITY gave us the inspiration for Level 2 and finally, LONDON made Level 3 possible. If zombies destroy humanity, these three cities will be the first to suffer the zombies rage.
